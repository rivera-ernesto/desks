# Keeping usage low

A paid plan is a weekly allowance. These habits decide whether it lasts the week. The numbers come
from the author's own measured sessions, not from a benchmark; treat them as one person's evidence.

- **Long conversations are the main cost.** Every reply re-reads the whole conversation. In one
  measured week, about two thirds of the top model's usage sat on turns past 200,000 tokens of
  context. Close at milestones and start fresh from the README (`reference/close.md`).
- **Screenshots are the heaviest thing a conversation can hold.** One session used over a third of
  a week's allowance; 70% of its context was 320 screenshots and the conversation itself was 3%.
  Extraction goes to `desk-screen`.
- **The top model is for the conversation and the judgment.** Reading, searching, extracting and
  first drafts go to a named worker on a cheaper model. A subagent with no name inherits your
  model and saves nothing.
- **Read on demand.** Open a file when the task needs it. A boot that reads everything "to be
  safe" spends the allowance before the work starts.
- **Show the change, not the document.** When revising, give the changed lines. The full text goes
  out once, when it is final.
- **Size the answer to the decision.** A yes-or-no question gets a short answer with its reason.
- **Reviews cost nothing here.** The cross-vendor review runs on other vendors' free models and
  does not touch the Claude allowance, so it is not something to ration.
- **If usage is high** (the user says so, or a limit warning appears), say it once at the start,
  prefer workers even for small reads, and propose the close earlier. Never refuse to work.
