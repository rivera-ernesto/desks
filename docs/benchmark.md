# Comparing Desk with the setup you already have

For someone who already uses AI tools heavily and wants to know whether Desk adds anything. It is
a protocol, not a result: nobody has run it yet. One person, one week, real tasks.

## What is being compared

- **A**: your current way of working, unchanged.
- **B**: Claude Code with Desk at its defaults, a free review key with the higher daily cap
  unlocked, and, on a Mac, the private lane.

The claim under test: *B gives decisions you trust more, for less of the Claude allowance, than A
does for the same tasks.* It can fail on either half.

## Tasks

Pick six real tasks from your own week, two of each kind, before you start:

1. **A decision with money in it** (a price, a purchase, a quote).
2. **A text other people will read** (a post, a proposal, a hard message).
3. **A research question** where being wrong has a cost.

Do one of each kind in A and one in B. Decide which goes where by coin toss, not by preference.

## What to write down, per task

| Measure | How |
|---|---|
| Time to an answer you would act on | minutes, by the clock |
| Corrections you had to make | count the times you said "no, that is wrong" or redid something |
| Errors found before acting | in B: findings adopted from the review files. In A: whatever your own checks caught |
| Errors found after acting | fill in a week later; this is the measure that matters most |
| Claude allowance used | the usage percentage before and after the session |
| Other spend | what the task cost in your other subscriptions, roughly |
| Would you do it this way again | yes / no, and one line why |

## Reading the result

- If B's reviews never changed an answer, the loop is ceremony for your kind of work. Say so.
- If B used more allowance than A for the same result, the workers and the close are not paying
  for themselves.
- If your existing subscriptions, plugged in as review lanes (`desk adopt`), beat the free models
  clearly, that is a finding too: the pattern may be worth more than the free tier it ships with.

Six tasks is a small sample. Treat the outcome as a reason to look closer, not as proof.
