# Packet: asking price for a used entry-level camera kit

## Context

A person wants to sell a fifteen-year-old entry-level DSLR with its kit lens, a bag and a memory
card, in working order, in a mid-size city with a small second-hand market. They want it gone
within a month and do not want to negotiate for weeks.

## Proposed answer

List at 120, accept 90. Comparable kits are listed locally between 120 and 140. Say the shutter
count is unknown. Post on a weekend morning, when more people browse.

## What to check

- Is 120 a sound anchor?
- Is the weekend claim supported by anything?
- What is missing from the plan?
