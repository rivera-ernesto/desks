## What we learned

- Sold listings, not asking prices, set the anchor. The first draft priced from asking prices and
  was 25% too high. (2026-03-14)
- An unknown shutter count reads as a hidden problem. Stating the number, even a high one, is
  better than "unknown". (2026-03-14)
- Bundling the bag and card adds almost nothing to the price; it shortens the conversation.
  (2026-03-14)
